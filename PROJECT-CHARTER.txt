===============================================================
 DEEZ PLANTS - THE CHARTER
 Who does what, where each fact lives, and what may never happen
 Written 18 Sep 2026
===============================================================

This is the stable document. The app's screens change; this does
not. Give it to the AI project alongside GPT-PROJECT-BRIEF.txt
and gpt-prompt.txt.

It exists because four parties touch this project and each keeps
reaching for the others' job. Written down once, nobody has to
re-derive it.


===============================================================
 1. WHAT THE PROJECT IS FOR
===============================================================

The owner's own words, after using it for a week:

  "What works ... this is really the whole point of the app, to
  track what works and make it how I take care of it and see it
  thrive. Together with AI and my observations and actions over
  time I can use this app to remember all the things I can't."

Everything below follows from that sentence. The app is a memory,
not an adviser. It holds what happened so that a person - helped
by an AI - can notice what they would otherwise forget.

22 real houseplants. One phone. No server, no accounts, no sync.


===============================================================
 2. THE FOUR ROLES
===============================================================

--- THE OWNER ------------------------------------------------

THE ONLY ONE WHO CAN LOOK AT A PLANT. That is not a courtesy; it
is the reason the other three exist in the shape they do.

  Owns:     health ratings, notes_user, every approval, every
            decision about what the plants need
  Decides:  whether any proposed change is right
  Never:    has to justify a rejection to anyone

Health is a human judgement made by looking. No amount of care
history implies it, and nothing else in this system may write it
except a proposal the owner accepts.


--- THE APP --------------------------------------------------

DECIDES NOTHING AND REMEMBERS EVERYTHING.

  Owns:     the append-only event log, and every number derived
            from replaying it
  Does:     record what happened; show it back; make care one tap
  Never:    computes health. Never says a plant needs water.
            Never edits an entry - a correction is a new entry.

Everything it shows - adherence, due dates, needs-attention,
calendars, history - is derived by replaying the log from the
beginning. Nothing derived is stored as fact, so the record can
never disagree with itself.


--- THE AI (the "plant bot") ---------------------------------

NOTICES AND RECALLS. DOES NOT DECIDE.

  Owns:     nothing
  Does:     species knowledge the owner has not got; contra-
            dictions he has not spotted; what he said six months
            ago that bears on today; a current care priority
  May:      propose changes to the fields listed in gpt-prompt.txt
  Never:    applies anything. Never proposes a derived value.
            Never touches notes_user. Never infers health from a
            watering calendar.

Its most valuable output is often "there is not enough here to
say". That is a real contribution and is never treated as failure.


--- CLAUDE CODE ----------------------------------------------

BUILDS, AND WRITES DOWN WHY.

  Owns:     the code, the specification, the handoff
  Does:     build, test, and record the reasoning behind every
            decision
  Never:    changes a rule without the owner's word, and never
            quietly

A conversation ends and takes everything unwritten with it. So a
decision that is not in NEXT_SESSION_HANDOFF.md did not happen.


===============================================================
 3. WHERE EACH FACT LIVES - ONE PLACE EACH
===============================================================

The single most useful rule here: WHEN TWO PLACES HOLD THE SAME
FACT, THEY WILL DRIFT, AND NOBODY WILL KNOW WHICH IS RIGHT.

  The plants themselves     the app, on the phone. Nowhere else.
  Species knowledge         the app's six reference fields, and
                            they travel in every manifest
  What was said on a walk   the transcript, attached to the walk
  What the app is FOR       this file
  How the data is shaped    FIELD_DEFINITIONS.md
  What the AI may propose   gpt-prompt.txt
  How the AI should read
    a package               GPT-PROJECT-BRIEF.txt
  Why anything is the
    way it is               NEXT_SESSION_HANDOFF.md
  Progress and what is left the build log page
  The code                  github.com/deez-plants/deez-plants.github.io

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE AI PROJECT: do not load plant-specific
reference material into it. The app already holds that, and the
manifest carries current values every round - a copy frozen in
project instructions goes stale the first time the owner edits a
field. General material that is not about THESE plants - a
propagation guide, a pest key - does not conflict and is welcome.


===============================================================
 4. THE RULES THAT DO NOT BEND
===============================================================

Enforced in code. A file breaking one is rejected whole.

 1. THE APP NEVER COMPUTES HEALTH, and neither does the AI by
    arithmetic. A health proposal must name what was SEEN -
    colour, posture, rot, pests - and may never come from
    watering history.

 2. ADHERENCE IS COUNTS AND DAYS, NEVER A SCORE OUT OF TEN.
    "On time 14 of 18, average 2 days late." Never 7.8/10.

 3. notes_user AND collection_notes_user ARE THE OWNER'S ALONE.
    An update file naming either is rejected outright.

 4. EVERY PROPOSED CHANGE IS SEEN AND INDIVIDUALLY REJECTABLE.
    Batch selection is allowed - the argument happens in conver-
    sation before the file exists - but rows arrive UNSELECTED
    and any one can be unticked.

 5. ENTRIES ARE APPEND-ONLY. A mistake is taken back with a Void
    entry naming it; both stay, neither counts. Never a deletion:
    a deleted entry could return from a backup with no record of
    the intent to remove it.

 6. AN INTERVAL PASSING IS NEVER PROOF A PLANT NEEDS WATER. It is
    a fact about the calendar and a prompt to look. Nobody -
    app, AI or document - may render it as an instruction.

 7. WHERE THE WORDS AND THE MARKERS DISAGREE, THE WORDS WIN. A
    marker says which page was open, not what was discussed.

 8. THE AUDIO IS GROUND TRUTH; THE CLOCK IS AN ESTIMATE. Where
    they disagree about how long a walk was, believe the audio.

 9. A WALK'S AUDIO IS DELETED once its transcript is attached AND
    ITS COVERAGE PASSES - and not one moment before. That
    condition has twice refused to delete a recording whose
    transcript came back incomplete.

10. DERIVED STATE IS REBUILT, NEVER PATCHED. It is 22 plants.


===============================================================
 5. THE CYCLE
===============================================================

  1. The owner logs care as it happens. It counts immediately.
  2. Now and then he records a walk, talking, naming plants.
  3. The laptop transcribes it. The words come back to the app;
     the audio goes.
  4. He builds a review package and hands it to the AI.
  5. They TALK. The AI reads, questions, is questioned.
  6. Only once they agree does the AI produce an update file.
  7. He reviews every row in the app and applies what he accepts.
  8. Since last time shows what moved.

Step 5 is the one that makes the rest work, and the one most
easily skipped. AN UPDATE FILE PRODUCED BEFORE THE CONVERSATION
HAS SETTLED IS THE WRONG SHAPE OF ARTEFACT.

Roughly monthly while the baseline is young, less often later,
with short targeted questions in between. A full 22-plant walk is
not required to ask about one plant.


===============================================================
 6. WHAT THIS SYSTEM CANNOT DO, AND WILL NOT PRETEND TO
===============================================================

TWENTY-TWO PLANTS AND A HANDFUL OF RATINGS A YEAR CANNOT SUPPORT
STATISTICAL INFERENCE. There is no significance to find here. A
confident wrong answer is worse than no answer, because the owner
would end up learning the arithmetic instead of his plants.

So on cause, the job is to HOLD THE EVIDENCE STILL: here is the
change, here is the rating before, here is the rating after, here
is how long between them, here are the photographs. Then stop.

It also cannot know anything about soil. Not from a calendar, not
from a photograph, not from a history. Only a finger in the pot
knows that, and only the owner has one.

And it cannot notify. A web app with no server cannot schedule a
reminder for later; that is a platform limit, not a missing
feature. A calendar file the phone subscribes to is the way
round it, and is not built yet.


===============================================================
 7. THE HABITS THAT KEEP IT HONEST
===============================================================

Learned the expensive way, each one more than once:

  - WHEN A MECHANISM CHANGES, THE COPY DESCRIBING IT IS PART OF
    THE CHANGE. Stale instructions have three times sent the
    owner to do work the app already did.

  - A NUMBER READ OFF A SCREEN BEATS A THEORY. Every recording
    fault in this project was found by the owner reporting two
    numbers that did not agree, and several confident diagnoses
    were wrong first.

  - ARITHMETIC THAT CANNOT BE TESTED WILL BE WRONG. One four-line
    sum was wrong three times while it lived tangled in module
    state. It is now its own file with its failures pinned.

  - A GUARD THAT REFUSES SOMETHING IS THE GUARD WORKING. Coverage
    failing and keeping the audio is not an error message.

  - WRITE THE REASONING, NOT JUST THE DECISION. The reasoning is
    what lets a later reader tell a deliberate choice from an
    accident, and stops it being undone by someone helpful.
