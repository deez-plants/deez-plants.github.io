===============================================================
 PART 1 - THE SHARED FOLDER (do this once)
===============================================================

We are using ONEDRIVE, not iCloud. Reason: OneDrive is already
installed, signed in and running on your laptop. iCloud would
mean installing and configuring something new for no gain.

THE FOLDERS ALREADY EXIST. On the laptop:

    C:\Users\604dr\OneDrive\Deez Plants\
        1 walks in      you drop exported walks here
        2 transcripts   the ONLY thing you ever import
        3 ai            packages out, AI updates back
        4 backups       Save my record / Save everything
        archive         markers and notes. Never audio.

They are numbered because sorted by name you would get ai,
archive, backups, transcripts, walks - the reverse of how you
use them. Numbers keep them in order on the phone too.

You only need to make your PHONE able to see them.


STEP 1.1 - Install OneDrive on your iPhone
-------------------------------------------
Open the App Store, search "OneDrive", install the blue cloud
one made by Microsoft. It is free.


STEP 1.2 - Sign in
-------------------------------------------
Open it and sign in with the SAME Microsoft account your laptop
uses. If you are not sure which that is, on the laptop click the
blue cloud in the bottom-right taskbar - it shows the email.


STEP 1.3 - Check you can see the folders
-------------------------------------------
On the phone, open the OneDrive app. You should see "Deez
Plants" with the five folders inside.

If you do NOT: the phone is on a different Microsoft account
than the laptop. Fix that before going further.


STEP 1.4 - Make it appear in the Files app
-------------------------------------------
This is what lets you SAVE into it from the app.

  Open the Files app on your phone
  Tap Browse (bottom right)
  Tap the three dots at the top right
  Tap Edit
  Turn ON the switch next to OneDrive
  Tap Done


STEP 1.5 - Worth 10 seconds: favourite two folders
-------------------------------------------
  In Files: Browse > OneDrive > Deez Plants
  Press and hold "1 walks in"    > Favourite
  Press and hold "2 transcripts" > Favourite

They then sit at the top of Browse forever, one tap from the
file picker. This is the difference between two taps and hunting
through folders every time.


===============================================================
 PART 2 - START THE WATCHER (once per session at the laptop)
===============================================================

The watcher is a small program that sits waiting. When a walk
arrives it unzips it, transcribes it, files everything, and
throws away the audio once the words are proven.


STEP 2.1 - Start it
-------------------------------------------
DOUBLE-CLICK the shortcut on your Desktop:

    Deez Plants - watch for walks

(Or start-watching.bat in the deez-plants folder - same thing.)

A black window opens and says:

    Watching C:\Users\604dr\OneDrive\Deez Plants\1 walks in
    Waiting...

That is it working. LEAVE THAT WINDOW OPEN.


STEP 2.2 - Does it have to be running all the time?
-------------------------------------------
No. It only needs to be running at the moment a walk arrives.

If you drop a walk in while it is NOT running, nothing is lost -
the zip just sits there. Start the watcher later and it picks it
up within ten seconds.


STEP 2.3 - Stopping it
-------------------------------------------
Close the window with the X. That is all.

It does NOT keep running in the background afterwards, and it
does not start itself when the laptop boots. If you ever want it
to, say so - it is a five-minute job.


===============================================================
 PART 3 - TRANSCRIBE A WALK (every time)
===============================================================

STEP 3.1 - On the phone, export the walk
-------------------------------------------
  Open the app
  Tap More, then Recordings
  Find the walk
  Tap "Export for Whisper"
  The Share sheet opens
  Tap "Save to Files"
  Choose OneDrive > Deez Plants > 1 walks in
  Tap Save

That is your part done.


STEP 3.2 - Wait
-------------------------------------------
Watch the black window. Within a minute or two:

    Found 2026-09-14 1833 walk 4.zip
      unzipped into 2026-09-14 1833 walk 4\
      transcribing...
      wrote 2 transcripts / 2026-09-14 1833 walk 4 - TRANSCRIPT.txt
      released 17.7 MB of audio - the words are the record now
      done.

THE MODEL IS ALREADY DOWNLOADED on your laptop, so there is no
long silent wait. Two minutes of walk takes well under a minute.


STEP 3.3 - Put it into the app (no copying needed)
-------------------------------------------
  Open the app
  More > Recordings
  Find THE SAME walk
  Tap "Add transcript"
  Tap the green "Choose the transcript file" button
  The picker opens on Recents - that is normal
  Tap "Browse" at the BOTTOM RIGHT
  Tap "2 transcripts" (favourited in step 1.5, so it is at the top)
  Tap the file ending "- TRANSCRIPT.txt"
  Tap Save

You do NOT need to open the file, select all, or copy anything.

The app then tells you what it did with the audio:

    "17.7 MB of audio released - the words are the record now"

or, if the coverage check did not pass:

    "The audio is kept, because coverage did not pass"


===============================================================
 THE GOLDEN RULE - WHY YOUR AUDIO DISAPPEARS
===============================================================

Your rule, 14 Sep: a walk's audio has done its job once the
words are in the app. Audio is about 1 MB a minute - one walk
came to 37 MB across its folder and its zip. The transcript is
what the AI reads, what the coverage check measures, and what
survives a backup.

So once a transcript is attached AND ITS COVERAGE PASSES, the
audio is deleted - on the phone, on the laptop, and it never
enters a backup.

THE COVERAGE CONDITION IS NOT FUSSINESS. On 14 Sep your walk 4
transcript failed coverage the first time, because the app had
the walk's length wrong. It had to be rebuilt FROM THE AUDIO,
twice. If the audio had been deleted the moment that first
transcript landed, seven minutes of that walk would have been
gone for good and nothing would have told you.

A transcript that fails coverage is exactly when the recording
is still needed. Those walks keep everything.


===============================================================
 PART 4 - THE AI ROUND-TRIP
===============================================================

This is the half of the app that has never been tested. It is
how the AI sees your record and proposes changes you approve.

STEP 4.0 - BACK UP FIRST
-------------------------------------------
  In the app: More > Back up > Save my record
  Save to Files > OneDrive > Deez Plants > 4 backups

You have never applied an update before. If anything goes odd,
this is the undo.


STEP 4.1 - Make the package
-------------------------------------------
  More > Prepare review package
  Save to Files > OneDrive > Deez Plants > 3 ai


STEP 4.2 - Hand it to your GPT plant bot
-------------------------------------------
SET THE PROJECT UP ONCE. Give it, as its instructions or
knowledge files:

    GPT-PROJECT-BRIEF.txt   (from PART 1 down)
    FIELD_DEFINITIONS.md
    gpt-prompt.txt

All three are in C:\Users\604dr\deez-plants

The brief tells it where this project got to, what the app
became, the rules it cannot break, and what you want settled
with it. Its FIRST reply should be its honest read of where you
are - not an update file. There are five open questions in it
for you to work through together.

DO NOT upload the source code. It is a React app; thousands of
lines about screens and almost nothing about plants. If it wants
to look at something specific, give it the GitHub link:
github.com/deez-plants/deez-plants.github.io

THEN, EACH TIME: upload the review package zip from
OneDrive > Deez Plants > 3 ai, and ask for an update file.

Attach photos to the chat by hand if you want an opinion on how
something LOOKS - photos are not in the package.

(You can also just ask ME on the laptop - I can read the folder
directly, no uploading. But you wanted the plant bot to do the
thinking, so this is the path.)


STEP 4.3 - Bring it back
-------------------------------------------
  In the app: More > Apply AI update
  Tap "Choose file"
  Pick the update file from OneDrive > Deez Plants > 3 ai

Same as the transcript - pick the file, no copying. There is a
paste box as well if you prefer it.

STEP 4.4 - Approve row by row
-------------------------------------------
Every proposed change is shown on its own with its reason. You
accept or reject EACH ONE. There is no apply-all button and
there never will be - that is deliberate.

Your own notes are never touched by any of this.


===============================================================
 PART 5 - IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
===============================================================

THE BLACK WINDOW SAYS "python is not recognized"
  Close it, reopen it, try again. If it persists, tell me.

THE WALK NEVER APPEARS ON THE LAPTOP
  OneDrive has not synced yet. Check the blue cloud icon in the
  taskbar - if it is spinning, wait. If it has a red X, OneDrive
  needs attention.

THE TRANSCRIPT NEVER APPEARS ON THE PHONE
  Same thing in reverse. Give OneDrive a minute.

THE TRANSCRIPT STOPS EARLY
  Look in the walk's folder for what-happened.txt and send me
  what it says. Do not try to work it out from the numbers - the
  walk keeps its own account now, and that is what I need.

IT SAYS COVERAGE: FAIL
  Read the report at the top of the transcript. Going quiet is
  no longer a failure, so if it fails now there is a real
  reason. Send me those lines.

ANYTHING ELSE
  Copy whatever the black window printed in RED and send it.
  Red is the error; the rest is noise.


===============================================================
 PART 6 - WHAT EACH FOLDER IS FOR
===============================================================

OneDrive\Deez Plants
  1 walks in      You drop exported walks here. The watcher
                  empties it as it works.

  2 transcripts   The file you import into the app. NOTHING
                  ELSE lives here, so you can never pick the
                  wrong thing.

  3 ai            Review packages out, AI update files back.

  4 backups       Save my record / Save everything.

  archive         Each walk's markers, screen log and its own
                  account of what happened. NEVER audio - see
                  the golden rule above. Small, and you will
                  rarely open it.

deez-plants\My_content_to_share    This guide. Nothing else needs to go here.

deez-plants-safety-copy-2026-09-14    A copy of everything as it stood before the folders were
    reorganised on 14 Sep, audio included. Delete it once you
    are happy nothing is missing.

ONE WARNING, and the watcher protects you from it:
Every export contains a file called markers.json. If you ever
unzip two walks into the SAME folder by hand, the second
overwrites the first - and the transcript then credits one
walk's words to the other walk's plants, with nothing anywhere
looking wrong. Let the watcher do the unzipping.
