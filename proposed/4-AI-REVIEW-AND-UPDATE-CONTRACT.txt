 Part 4 of 4 · what you may DO with the data

Status: DRAFT FOR AUDIT. Nothing has been installed, modified,
retired or deleted.

**THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT LIST FIELDS.** The Data & Package
Contract owns the field list and marks each field AI-PROPOSABLE,
OWNER ONLY or DERIVED. Read it there. Two documents enumerating
the same fields is how sources come to disagree, and this
project has been bitten by that already.

Here: how to read a package, what may be proposed and on what
evidence, how to write the file, and what can honestly be
concluded.


===============================================================
 1. READ IN THIS ORDER
===============================================================

1. THE TRANSCRIPT, IF THERE IS ONE. It is the only place the
   owner says WHY. Read it before the numbers, or the numbers
   will frame everything that follows.

2. THE MANIFEST. What each plant currently IS. The collection
   block is the roster.

3. THE EVENTS. What has happened since the last package.

4. THE MARKERS AND SCREEN LOG. Attribution, and only
   attribution.

5. ANY PHOTOGRAPHS. Flagged ones arrive named for their plant;
   hand-attached ones the owner will identify.

QUOTE THE OWNER'S OWN WORDS IN YOUR REASONS WHERE YOU CAN.
"You said on the 14th that the lower leaves were yellowing" is a
reason they can check. "Adherence suggests under-watering" is an
inference they cannot, and is forbidden besides.


===============================================================
 2. HOW TO READ WHAT YOU ARE GIVEN
===============================================================

ATTRIBUTION IS EVIDENCE OF WHAT WAS ON SCREEN, NOT PROOF OF WHAT
WAS DISCUSSED. The owner may be looking at one plant and talking
about the next. WHERE THE WORDS AND THE ATTRIBUTION DISAGREE,
THE WORDS WIN.

ON AN INTERRUPTED WALK, a marker's position in the transcript is
a FLOOR, not a point — see the Data & Package Contract. Never
treat raw offsets as one continuous timeline. Where a recording
reports a lot of background audio, lean harder on the words.

AN EMPTY EVENT LIST OR AN ABSENT WALK almost always means
"nothing new since the last package", not missing data. Entries
and walks are carried exactly once; the manifest repeats.

A VOID ENTRY takes back the entry it names. Neither counts. Read
the pair as "this did not happen".

A SILENT WALK IS NOT A HEALTHY WALK. Absence of comment is
absence of evidence.

AN OLD OBSERVATION IS HISTORY, NOT CURRENT CONDITION. Newer
evidence — a photograph, something said on a walk — supersedes
it. Say which you are relying on.

THE AUDIO NO LONGER EXISTS. Do not ask for it.


===============================================================
 3. WHAT YOU MAY PROPOSE, AND ON WHAT EVIDENCE
===============================================================

You may propose changes to the fields the Data & Package
Contract marks AI-PROPOSABLE. Nothing else exists for this
purpose. Anything it marks OWNER ONLY or DERIVED is outside this
contract entirely, and a file naming one is rejected whole.

**YOU CANNOT WRITE TO THE RECORD.** You produce a file; the
owner approves individual rows; the app writes entries. There is
no path by which a proposal becomes a change without a person.

THE REFERENCE FIELDS ARE THE REAL OPENING. Species knowledge the
record cannot produce — what a plant wants natively, when
repotting is worth it, what goes wrong with it, how it
propagates, what it does seasonally. This is the one part of the
job where a good general answer is genuinely useful. Keep each
to a short paragraph. Say which you are confident about and
which you are inferring from a genus rather than knowing.

WATERING INTERVALS ARE REFERENCES, NOT SCHEDULES. Propose them
as approximations that condition overrides. A winter interval
should normally be LONGER than its summer one; if you propose
them equal or shorter, say explicitly why for that plant, and
make sure it does not contradict what you wrote in that plant's
own seasonal reference.

HEALTH — THE NARROWEST RULE IN THIS DOCUMENT.

  You may propose a health value ONLY from something a schedule
  cannot show: colour, posture, rot, pests, new growth, damage —
  seen in a photograph or described by the owner.

  YOU MUST NAME WHAT YOU SAW, in the reason.

  YOU MAY NEVER DERIVE IT from watering history, adherence,
  elapsed intervals or counts. Health is the owner's judgement,
  made by looking at the plant. Provenance stays visible and
  they can overwrite you.

  Proposing none at all is a perfectly good answer.

THE CARE FOCUS is ONE current priority — about now, not about
the species, which belongs in the reference fields. Up to two
concise sentences. "Monitor only" is legitimate and often
correct. Do not write one for every plant for the sake of
completeness.

AN ELAPSED INTERVAL NEVER MEANS "WATER NOW". It is a fact about
the calendar and a prompt to look. Never write "needs water",
"overdue" or any phrasing that reads as an instruction about
soil you cannot see.

SHARED PLANTERS: propose intervals for members individually, as
species knowledge. Do not assume adherence will follow them
while they share a pot, and do not propose making them equal
unless the owner asks.


===============================================================
 4. TALK FIRST. THE FILE COMES LAST.
===============================================================

  1. Read the package and reply IN PROSE.
  2. The owner questions, disagrees, adds what you could not
     know.
  3. Only once that has settled, write the update file.

AN UPDATE FILE PRODUCED BEFORE THE CONVERSATION HAS SETTLED IS
THE WRONG SHAPE OF ARTEFACT. It asks the owner to adjudicate in
a review table what should have been argued in conversation.

This matters more since batch approval exists. The owner may now
select every row and untick the few they disagree with, which
only works if the argument already happened.


===============================================================
 5. THE UPDATE FILE
===============================================================

SHAPE

  {
    "package_id": "<the package you are answering>",
    "generated":  "<ISO date>",
    "changes": [
      { "plant_id": "<identifier>",
        "field":    "<an AI-PROPOSABLE field>",
        "value":    <typed per the Data & Package Contract>,
        "reason":   "<specific, non-empty>" }
    ],
    "unaddressed": ["<identifier>", ...],
    "notes": "<optional, brief>"
  }

Care-instruction changes additionally carry an operation of
"add", or "replace" naming an existing item on that plant. There
is no delete.

COMPLETENESS
EVERY plant in the manifest appears in EXACTLY ONE of "changes"
or "unaddressed". Not both, not neither. This is what stops a
plant being silently skipped.

ONE CHANGE, ONE FIELD, ONE REASON
Never bundle two ideas into one row. A row carrying two forces
the owner to reject a good half along with a bad one.

REASONS
Non-empty, specific, and about THAT plant. Boilerplate repeated
across rows is a signal that the reasoning was not done —
the owner has already caught exactly that, and was right to.

PROPOSE NOTHING RATHER THAN SOMETHING THIN. Put the plant in
"unaddressed". A gap stated is worth more than a guess made.

DO NOT INVENT plants, identifiers or fields that are not in the
manifest.

SMALLER AND MORE FREQUENT BEATS LARGE AND SPECULATIVE.


===============================================================
 6. WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR FILE
===============================================================

REJECTED WHOLE, with the reason named, if any of these fail.
Nothing partial is ever applied:

  - the package identifier is one the app actually exported
  - no previous update has answered that same package
  - every plant identifier exists, and none is archived
  - every field is recognised, and none is OWNER ONLY or DERIVED
  - the owner's private notes are not named
  - values match their declared types; enums match exactly,
    including case; numeric ranges hold
  - a health change carries a reason naming a visual observation
  - care-instruction items carry a valid operation, are within
    length, and a "replace" names a real existing item
  - every plant appears in changes or unaddressed
  - every change carries a non-empty reason

FLAGGED, NOT REJECTED: if the owner edited that exact field
after the package was generated, the row is shown with their
value, their date and yours. They choose.

THE REVIEW TABLE shows every change with current value, proposed
value and your reason. ROWS ARRIVE UNSELECTED. The owner may
select all and untick, or tick individually. Nothing is applied
until they act.

ON APPLY, each accepted row becomes an entry recording that it
came from you and from which review. A row whose proposed value
equals the current value writes nothing. Rejections are recorded
as counts, not quietly discarded.

YOUR REASONING IS NOT STORED — only the change and its reason.
So the reason has to carry the argument on its own.


===============================================================
 7. WHAT YOU CAN HONESTLY CONCLUDE
===============================================================

READ THIS TWICE. It is the part most likely to go wrong.

A COLLECTION THIS SIZE, WITH A HANDFUL OF RATINGS A YEAR, CANNOT
SUPPORT STATISTICAL INFERENCE. There is no significance to find.
A confident wrong answer is actively worse than no answer,
because the owner would end up learning your arithmetic instead
of their plants.

YOUR JOB ON CAUSE IS TO HOLD THE EVIDENCE STILL: here is the
change, here is the rating before, here is the rating after,
here is how long between them, here are the photographs. Then
stop. The judgement belongs to the person who looked at the
plant.

THINGS YOU CAN DO WELL, AND SHOULD:

  - Notice what they have not. "You have said 'not sure what to
    do' about this plant on three separate walks."
  - Point at contradictions. "The care spec says bright
    indirect; you moved it somewhere you described as dark."
  - Recall what they cannot. Something said months ago that
    bears on today.
  - Supply species knowledge they do not have.
  - Say plainly when there is not enough to go on.
  - **Say when you think the app, the data or these documents
    are wrong.** This is a right, not a tolerance. Your first
    real review found two genuine defects, which has been the
    most valuable thing you have produced.

THINGS YOU MUST NOT DO:

  - Rank changes by effect size, or badge any as having worked.
  - Average ratings into a number.
  - Claim causation from two data points.
  - Fill a gap with a plausible guess rather than naming it as a
    gap.
  - Treat an elapsed interval as evidence about soil.
  - Derive health from anything other than observation.

MARK UNCERTAINTY. Say VERIFY. An unmarked guess is the one
failure this whole arrangement is built to prevent.
