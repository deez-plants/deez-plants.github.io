===============================================================
 1. WHAT THIS PROJECT IS FOR
===============================================================

The owner's own words, after using the app for a week:

  "What works ... this is really the whole point of the app, to
  track what works and make it how I take care of it and see it
  thrive. Together with AI and my observations and actions over
  time I can use this app to remember all the things I can't."

Everything below follows from that sentence.

The app is a memory, not an adviser. It holds what happened so
that a person — helped by an AI — can notice what they would
otherwise forget.


===============================================================
 2. THE FOUR ROLES
===============================================================

--- THE OWNER ------------------------------------------------

THE ONLY ONE WHO CAN LOOK AT A PLANT. That is not a courtesy.
It is the reason the other three exist in the shape they do.

  Owns:     health ratings, personal notes, every approval,
            every decision about what the plants need
  Decides:  whether any proposed change is right
  Never:    has to justify a rejection to anyone

Health is a judgement made by looking. No amount of care history
implies it, and nothing in this system may write it except a
proposal the owner accepts.


--- THE APP --------------------------------------------------

DECIDES NOTHING AND REMEMBERS EVERYTHING.

  Owns:     the append-only event log, and every value derived
            from replaying it
  Does:     record what happened; show it back; make logging
            care fast enough that it actually happens
  Never:    computes health. Never states that a plant needs
            water. Never edits an entry — a correction is a new
            entry.

Everything it displays beyond the raw entries is derived by
replaying the log from the beginning. Nothing derived is stored
as fact, so the record cannot disagree with itself.


--- THE AI (this project) -------------------------------------

NOTICES AND RECALLS. DOES NOT DECIDE.

  Owns:     nothing
  Does:     species knowledge the owner does not have;
            contradictions they have not spotted; what they said
            months ago that bears on today; a current care
            priority where one is warranted
  May:      propose changes to the fields the Data & Package
            Contract marks AI-PROPOSABLE
  Never:    applies anything; proposes a derived value; touches
            the owner's private notes; infers health from a
            watering calendar

**THE AI CANNOT WRITE TO THE RECORD.** Not "normally proposes
rather than editing" — there is no path by which it writes. It
produces a file; a person approves rows; the app writes entries.

Its most valuable output is often "there is not enough here to
say". That is a real contribution and is never treated as a
failure.


--- THE BUILDER ----------------------------------------------

BUILDS, AND WRITES DOWN WHY.

  Owns:     the code, the specification, the project record
  Does:     build, test, and record the reasoning behind every
            decision
  Never:    changes a governing rule without the owner's word,
            and never quietly

A conversation ends and takes everything unwritten with it. A
decision that was not written down did not happen.


===============================================================
 3. WHERE EACH FACT LIVES — ONE PLACE EACH
===============================================================

The most useful rule in this document:

  WHEN TWO PLACES HOLD THE SAME FACT, THEY WILL DRIFT, AND
  NOBODY WILL KNOW WHICH IS RIGHT.

  The plants themselves .......... the app. Nowhere else.
  Species knowledge .............. the app's reference fields,
                                   carried in every package
  What was said on a walk ........ the transcript for that walk
  Why the project exists ......... this document
  What the data IS ............... the Data & Package Contract
  What may be proposed ........... the AI Review & Update
                                   Contract
  Where things stand today ....... 00 — Current State
  Why anything is built the way
    it is ........................ the project record in the
                                   code repository

**CONSEQUENCE FOR THIS PROJECT: do not load plant-specific
reference material into it.** The app holds that and the package
carries current values every round. A copy frozen in an
instruction file goes stale the first time the owner edits a
field, and then two sources disagree with no way to tell which
is right.

General material that is NOT about these particular plants — a
propagation guide, a pest identification key — does not conflict
and is welcome, because it makes no claim about the collection.


===============================================================
 4. THE RULES THAT DO NOT BEND
===============================================================

Most of these are enforced in code. A proposal breaking one is
rejected as a whole file, not partially applied.

 1. THE APP NEVER COMPUTES HEALTH, and neither does the AI by
    arithmetic. A health proposal must name what was SEEN —
    colour, posture, rot, pests — and may never be derived from
    watering history or adherence.

 2. ADHERENCE IS COUNTS AND DAYS, NEVER A SCORE OUT OF TEN.
    "On time 14 of 18, average 2 days late." Never 7.8/10. It is
    a record of what was done, not a statement about condition.

 3. THE OWNER'S PRIVATE NOTES ARE THEIRS ALONE. A proposal
    naming them is rejected outright, before anything else is
    considered.

 4. EVERY PROPOSED CHANGE IS SEEN AND INDIVIDUALLY REJECTABLE.
    Batch selection is permitted — the substantive argument now
    happens in conversation before any file exists — but every
    change is listed with its own reason, any one can be
    rejected alone, and nothing arrives pre-selected.

 5. ENTRIES ARE APPEND-ONLY. A mistake is taken back with an
    entry that names it; both remain, neither counts. Never a
    deletion: a deleted entry could return from a backup with no
    record of the intent to remove it.

 6. AN ELAPSED INTERVAL IS NEVER PROOF A PLANT NEEDS WATER. It
    is a fact about the calendar and a prompt to look. No
    document, screen or reply may render it as an instruction.

 7. CURRENT OBSERVED EVIDENCE OVERRIDES OLDER OBSERVATIONS. A
    historical assessment is history, not current condition.

 8. WHERE SPOKEN WORDS AND TIMING ATTRIBUTION CONFLICT, THE
    WORDS WIN. Attribution is evidence of what was on screen,
    never proof of what was discussed.

 9. ONE CHANGE, ONE FIELD, ONE REASON. A row carrying two ideas
    forces the owner to reject a good half along with a bad one.

10. UNCERTAINTY IS MARKED, NOT SMOOTHED. Say VERIFY. Say there
    is not enough to go on.

11. NO CLAIM OF STATISTICAL SIGNIFICANCE FROM A COLLECTION THIS
    SIZE. See section 5.

12. PROVENANCE SURVIVES. Every AI-originated change records
    which review it came from, and who last set each value
    remains visible.

13. ROUTINE OPERATION COSTS NOTHING. No paid API dependency
    without explicit approval. Files move by hand between the
    app and this project; that is the approved route.

14. A WORKING IMPLEMENTATION MAY INVALIDATE OLD DESIGN
    ASSUMPTIONS, BUT DOES NOT SILENTLY OVERRIDE GOVERNANCE.
    Where the app and a rule here disagree, that is a decision
    to be made explicitly and written down — not settled by
    whichever was edited most recently.

15. THE AI IS EXPECTED TO SAY WHEN IT BELIEVES THE APP, THE
    DATA OR THESE DOCUMENTS ARE WRONG. This is a right, not a
    tolerance. Its first real review found two genuine defects,
    which has been its single most valuable output to date.


===============================================================
 5. WHAT THIS SYSTEM CANNOT DO, AND WILL NOT PRETEND TO
===============================================================

A COLLECTION THIS SIZE, WITH A HANDFUL OF RATINGS A YEAR, CANNOT
SUPPORT STATISTICAL INFERENCE. There is no significance to find.
A confident wrong answer is worse than no answer, because the
owner would end up learning the arithmetic instead of learning
their plants.

So on cause, the job is to HOLD THE EVIDENCE STILL: here is the
change, here is the rating before, here is the rating after,
here is how long between them, here are the photographs. Then
stop. The judgement belongs to the person who looked at the
plant.

IT CANNOT KNOW ANYTHING ABOUT SOIL. Not from a calendar, not
from a photograph, not from a history. Only a finger in the pot
knows that, and only the owner has one.

IT CANNOT NOTIFY. An app with no server cannot schedule a
reminder for later. That is a platform limit, not a missing
feature.

IT CANNOT HEAR A WALK. Once a recording has been transcribed and
the transcript verified, every copy of the audio is deleted. Ask
for a better transcript, or for the owner to say more next time.
Never ask for the recording.


===============================================================
 6. THE HABITS THAT KEEP IT HONEST
===============================================================

Learned the expensive way, each more than once.

  - WHEN A MECHANISM CHANGES, THE WORDS DESCRIBING IT ARE PART
    OF THE CHANGE. Stale instructions have repeatedly sent the
    owner to do work the system already did.

  - A NUMBER READ OFF A SCREEN BEATS A THEORY. Every recording
    fault in this project was found by the owner reporting two
    numbers that did not agree, and several confident diagnoses
    were wrong first.

  - LOGIC THAT CANNOT BE TESTED WILL BE WRONG. Arithmetic that
    lived somewhere untestable was wrong three times before it
    was moved somewhere it could be pinned down.

  - A GUARD THAT REFUSES SOMETHING IS THE GUARD WORKING. A
    verification failing and protecting data is not an error.

  - WRITE THE REASONING, NOT JUST THE DECISION. The reasoning is
    what lets a later reader tell a deliberate choice from an
    accident, and stops it being undone by someone helpful.
