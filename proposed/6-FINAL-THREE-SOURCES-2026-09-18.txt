===============================================================
 00 — PLANT BOT — CURRENT STATE
 Version V4 · 18 Sep 2026 · replaces V3 (11 Aug 2026)
 PROJECT SOURCE 1 · orientation only
===============================================================

This is orientation. It says what the system is, how the cycle
runs, and where things stand. IT CONTAINS NO PLANT DATA — no
counts, no names, no identities, no condition, no care values.
Those live in the app and arrive in every review package.

The test for this document: if a sentence would become wrong
when the owner adds a plant, waters something, or edits a field,
it does not belong here.


WHAT THE APP IS

A local-first web app, installed to an iPhone home screen. It
logs plant care, holds health ratings the owner makes by
looking, records spoken walks, and exchanges review files with
this project.

No server. No account. No sync. Storage is per web address, so
the phone and any other device hold entirely separate records
that never converge on their own. A file carried by hand is the
only bridge.

Routine operation costs nothing and depends on no paid API.


WHERE THE DATA LIVES

One phone, in browser storage.

There is no copy anywhere this project can reach. Everything the
AI will ever see arrives in a review package the owner built and
handed over deliberately.

THE RECORD IS AN APPEND-ONLY EVENT LOG. Nothing is edited or
deleted; a correction is a new entry and both remain. Everything
else — adherence, due dates, what needs attention, calendars —
is derived by replaying that log, and none of it is stored as
fact.

The manifest inside a package is the authoritative roster of
what exists right now. Not this document, and not any list.


THE CYCLE

  1. The owner logs care as it happens. It counts immediately.
  2. Now and then they record a walk, talking, naming plants.
  3. A laptop transcribes it. The transcript is attached to the
     walk in the app, and ONLY ONCE IT IS ATTACHED AND ITS
     COVERAGE CHECK PASSES is the audio deleted.
  4. They build a review package and bring it here.
  5. WE TALK. Read, question, be questioned.
  6. Only once we agree does an update file get written.
  7. They review every proposed change in the app and apply
     what they accept.
  8. The app shows them what moved.

STEP 5 IS THE ONE THAT MAKES THE REST WORK, and the one most
easily skipped. An update file produced before the conversation
has settled is the wrong shape of artefact.

Rhythm: roughly monthly while the record is young, less often
later, with short targeted questions in between. A full walk of
the whole collection is not required to ask about one plant.


CURRENT STATUS

THE CORE PHONE WORKFLOW IS BUILT, INSTALLED AND IN DAILY USE.

The capture path is complete and tested on real walks: recording
with interruptions, transcription, coverage verification,
attaching the words, and releasing the audio once verified.

The review path has been exercised end to end at least once, in
both directions: a package built and handed over, a discussion
held, an update file produced, and its proposed changes reviewed
and applied in the app.

Entries count the moment they are logged. Packages carry each
entry and each walk exactly once, and are only marked as sent
once the owner confirms the file saved.

The current phase is not feature work. It is SOURCE AND CONTRACT
RECONCILIATION, followed by ordinary use.


NEXT MAJOR ACTION

1. FINAL CONTRACT AND SOURCE RECONCILIATION.
   Agree this set, let ChatGPT write the Project Instructions,
   install the agreed sources, and retire what they replace —
   preserving anything durable from the retired material first.

2. THEN A FULL REAL CYCLE ON THE NEW CONTRACT.
   Ordinary care logging over a period of normal use, a recorded
   walk, a review package built under the new contract, a
   discussion, an update file, and applied changes — so the
   contract is tested by use rather than by reading.

NO NEW FEATURE WORK IS CURRENTLY APPROVED BEFORE THE NEXT FULL
REAL REVIEW CYCLE. Bug fixes, validator alignment and contract
corrections are not blocked.


WHAT CHANGED RECENTLY, AND MATTERS HERE

Only things that alter how this project should read a package.
Mechanics belong in the Data & Package Contract.

  - Care entries count the moment they are logged. A package's
    derived values and its own entry list now describe the same
    world. If they ever disagree, that is a defect and saying so
    loudly is the correct response.

  - Entries and walks are carried by exactly one package each.
    The manifest is exempt and always states current values. An
    empty entry list usually means "nothing new", not missing
    data.

  - Photos can be flagged to travel inside a package under the
    filename the record knows them by, so identity needs no
    guessing.

  - An interrupted walk is several recordings, and its markers
    carry the translation between the walk's clock and the
    transcript's timeline.

  - Health remains the owner's judgement. The AI may propose a
    value only from visible evidence, and must name what it saw.


WHAT IS NOT BUILT

REMINDERS / SCHEDULED NOTIFICATIONS.
IMPLEMENTATION FACT: the present local-only PWA has no built
scheduled notification system, and none is currently in
progress. This work remains DEFERRED AND OPEN. The direction
currently under discussion is a catch-up summary shown when the
app is opened, plus a calendar file the phone can subscribe to
so the operating system performs the notifying. Neither has been
built, and the approach is not final.

A DESK CONSOLE — a wide review table for a laptop. Deliberately
last, and under question, because the record lives on the phone
and a second device can only show what has been imported into
it.

A GENERAL CORRECTION TOOL. There is deliberately none beyond
superseding a field and taking back an entry moments after
writing it. An older mistake stays in the record and is
discussed rather than edited away.


VERIFY — OPEN AT TIME OF DRAFTING

[VERIFY] Whether the phone keeps recording while the app is
backgrounded appears to depend on the kind of interruption — one
walk kept recording through it, another stopped within two
seconds. Untested. The package reports the width of this
uncertainty per recording, so attribution degrades visibly
rather than silently.

[VERIFY] The text limits in the Data & Package Contract are
FINAL CONTRACT TARGETS that the importer does not yet enforce.
That document states current enforcement and target separately.
Until they are built, honour them as conventions.

===============================================================
 PLANT BOT — DATA & PACKAGE CONTRACT
 Version V1 · 18 Sep 2026 · from the current code
 PROJECT SOURCE 2 · what the data IS
===============================================================

THIS DOCUMENT OWNS THE IDENTIFIER AND FIELD LISTS. The AI Review
& Update Contract describes what may be done with them and
points here for what they are. The two must never both
enumerate — that is how two sources come to disagree.

This is a data contract, not a build specification. The app's
full specification stays in its code repository and is not a
source for this project.


===============================================================
 1. IDENTIFIERS
===============================================================

PLANT IDENTIFIER

  Format:   NNN-XXX — three digits, a hyphen, three UPPERCASE
            letters. Validated on registration.

  **THE COMPLETE IDENTIFIER IS PERMANENT AND IMMUTABLE AFTER
  REGISTRATION, SUFFIX INCLUDED.** This is settled, not open.

  It is the key of the plant's baseline record, which is written
  once and never updated, and no editable field reaches any part
  of it.

  RE-IDENTIFICATION CHANGES name AND species AS APPROPRIATE,
  NEVER THE IDENTIFIER. The identifier records which physical
  plant this is, not what it was once thought to be.

  NEVER REUSED. Numbers are assigned once and never reused,
  including after a plant is archived. A new plant takes the
  next unused number, counting past archived ones.

  Note for readers of older material: a previous source said the
  descriptive suffix could change on re-identification. That is
  superseded by the rule above.

PACKAGE IDENTIFIER

  Format:   PKG-YYYY-MM-DD-N — N counts packages built that day
  Minted:   when a package is built
  Used:     an update file must cite the package it answers;
            one package can be answered only once

WALK IDENTIFIER

  Format:   SES-YYYY-MM-DD-N — N counts walks started that day
  Minted:   when the walk STARTS, not when it is saved, so every
            entry logged during it carries the walk it belongs to

ENTRY IDENTIFIER

  Format:   EV-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-DEVICE-RANDOM
  Semantics:
    - unique across devices without coordination, which is what
      makes two records safe to merge by union;
    - roughly chronological when sorted, but ORDER IS NEVER
      TAKEN FROM THE IDENTIFIER — the fold sorts by date, then
      time, then identifier as a deterministic tiebreak;
    - never reused; an entry is never rewritten.

INSTRUCTION ITEM IDENTIFIER

  Format:   INS-YYYY-MM-DD-N
  Used:     to name the item a "replace" operation supersedes.

MEDIA IDENTIFIER

  Format:   NNN-XXX_YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_NN.jpg
  Both the identifier and the filename a flagged photo travels
  under, which is what makes identity require no guessing.


===============================================================
 2. THE RECORD
===============================================================

TWO LAYERS

  BASELINE  the plant as it entered the world. Written once at
            registration and NEVER updated.
  ENTRIES   every later change, as an append-only log.

A plant's current state is the baseline with every applicable
entry replayed over it in order. Nothing is stored twice, so
nothing can disagree with itself.

ENTRY TYPES

  care      Water · Feed · Repot · Photo · Inspect · Support ·
            Pest treat · Top-dress · Soil flush · Took cuttings ·
            Hard prune · Dead leaves · Trim back · Rotate ·
            Wipe leaves · Mist · Other
            plus RETIRED "Prune" — historical entries remain
            readable; it can no longer be chosen
  Rate      a health value
  Edit      one field, from → to
  Archive   retires a plant, carrying a reason
  Void      takes back an entry written moments earlier

ORDER is date, then time, then entry identifier.

READING A VOID: it names the entry it takes back. Derived state
skips BOTH. History shows both. Read the pair as "the entry it
names did not happen" — never as two events.

CORRECTION, IN FULL

  1. SUPERSEDING — a later Edit on the same field wins. Both
     entries remain.
  2. VOID — only for an entry written moments before, from the
     screen that wrote it.
  3. NOTHING ELSE. A mistaken entry older than that stays.

ARCHIVED PLANTS
An Archive entry, never a flag. Archived plants are absent from
the plant list in a package, named in its collection block with
their reason, and NOT eligible for any proposed change. Their
identifiers are permanent and never reused.

ENTRIES COUNT IMMEDIATELY. Derived values are rebuilt from the
whole log on every read. A package's derived values and its own
entry list therefore describe the same world; if they ever
disagree, that is a defect worth reporting loudly.

SNAPSHOTS are local comparison points, taken when a package is
built or on demand. They never appear in a package.


===============================================================
 3. THE FIELDS
===============================================================

Columns: meaning · type · limit · who may edit · live truth or
reference knowledge.

"AI-PROPOSABLE" means the AI Review & Update Contract's rules
may propose a change. It never means the AI can write it.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 3.1 AI-PROPOSABLE — editable by the owner AND proposable
--------------------------------------------------------------

species
  ACCEPTED PLANT IDENTIFICATION. Use the botanical species when
  confirmed; otherwise use the most specific supported
  identification without pretending greater certainty.
  string · see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

water_interval_days
  Summer reference interval in days. A REFERENCE, not a
  schedule: condition overrides it always.
  integer · 1–60 ENFORCED · owner + AI · reference

water_interval_days_winter
  Winter reference interval, or null for no seasonal change.
  integer or null · 1–60 ENFORCED · owner + AI · reference

light
  TERSE SPECIES LIGHT TARGET. What this plant wants.
  e.g. "Bright indirect light"
  NOT where it currently sits — that is room and spot, which are
  owner-only.
  string or null · see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

soil
  TERSE SUBSTRATE TARGET. What it should be potted in.
  e.g. "Free-draining, bark and perlite"
  string or null · see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

feed
  COMPACT USABLE INSTRUCTION. Enough to act on without reading
  a paragraph. e.g. "Balanced feed at half strength, monthly in
  active growth"
  string or null · see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

environment
  THE NATIVE HABITAT OR CLIMATE BEING APPROXIMATED. Concise.
  What this species evolved in, so the owner can judge how far
  their home is from it.
  **NOT THE CURRENT PLACEMENT.** Do not describe the flat, the
  room, the window or the light it actually gets. Placement is
  room and spot, which are owner-only and never duplicated here.
  string or null · see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

repotting
pruning
pests
season
propagation
  CONCISE SPECIES REFERENCE INFORMATION. When repotting is worth
  it and what it likes to sit in; what and when to cut; what
  goes wrong with this species and what it looks like; what it
  does seasonally, including dormancy; how it propagates.
  string or null · see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

health
  Condition, as a human judgement. Applied as a Rate entry, not
  an Edit.
  integer 1–10 ENFORCED · owner + AI UNDER STRICT CONDITIONS
  (see the AI Review & Update Contract) · LIVE TRUTH

status_label
  Stable · Improving · Declining · Needs attention
  enum, matched EXACTLY including case · owner + AI · live truth

do_next   — displayed in the app as AI CARE FOCUS
  ONE CURRENT PRIORITY, UP TO TWO CONCISE SENTENCES. About now,
  not about the species. "Monitor only" is legitimate and often
  correct.
  string or null · see 3.5 · owner + AI · live truth

care_instructions
  A LIST of standalone instruction items, each carrying its own
  provenance and identifier. Not a prose blob and not a single
  editable string.

  HOW IT WORKS, PRECISELY:
    - its CURRENT VALUE is DERIVED — the list is folded from the
      entries that added or replaced items, so it is rebuilt
      from the log like everything else;
    - it is nonetheless AI-PROPOSABLE, through its own
      operations rather than by supplying a new whole value;
    - operations are "add", or "replace" naming an existing
      item's identifier on that plant;
    - THERE IS NO DELETE through a proposal. Removal is the
      owner's alone.

  So: derived in how it is READ, proposable in how it is
  CHANGED. It is never set as a scalar.
  see 3.5 · owner + AI · reference

--------------------------------------------------------------
 3.2 OWNER ONLY — a proposal naming one is REJECTED WHOLE
--------------------------------------------------------------

name                    what the owner calls it
acquired                when it arrived
room                    which room — PLACEMENT
spot                    where in the room, in their words —
                        PLACEMENT
pot                     the container
planter                 which planter, or none
notes_user              their own notes on this plant
collection_notes_user   their notes on the collection
hero_media              which photograph represents the plant
compare_media           which two photographs are compared

THE TWO NOTE FIELDS ARE SINGLED OUT IN CODE: a file naming
either is rejected before anything else is considered. Read them
for context; never propose against them.

ROOM AND SPOT ARE THE PLACEMENT RECORD. Never restate placement
inside environment or light.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 3.3 DERIVED — never writable, never proposable
--------------------------------------------------------------

archived · archived_date · archived_reason
  From the Archive entry.

health_source       who last set the value — owner or AI
health_confirmed    when the rating was last confirmed
health_changed      when the value last actually MOVED
health_stale        the rating is old enough to revisit

adherence           on · slip · behind
on_time_count       completed intervals met
care_count          completed intervals scored
avg_days_late       mean lateness of the LATE ones only, or null
interval_days       the interval in force today, season resolved
next_due            date
days_past           days past next_due; NEGATIVE = days remaining
last_checked        date of the most recent entry on that plant

planter_shared_water
  True when the planter's registry entry says one soak serves
  the group.

Confirming a rating without changing it is a real action: it
refreshes health_confirmed without moving health_changed. So
"confirmed recently, unchanged since much earlier" is a
meaningful and common state.

ADHERENCE IS COUNTS AND DAYS. Never out of ten, never read as a
statement about condition.

--------------------------------------------------------------
 3.4 SHARED PLANTERS — what the flag does NOT do
--------------------------------------------------------------

planter_shared_water affects exactly two things: a control that
selects the whole group at once, and a label.

IT AFFECTS NOTHING OPERATIONAL. Due dates, adherence, history
and calendars are strictly per plant, on each plant's own
interval. One soak writes one entry per member because both were
selected.

A planter-level interval was designed and then DROPPED by the
owner: each plant keeps its own schedule even sharing soil, and
the differing due dates from a single soak are accepted rather
than treated as a defect.

CONSEQUENCE: intervals may be proposed for members individually,
as species knowledge. Do not assume adherence will follow them
while they share a pot, and do not propose making them equal
unless the owner asks.

===============================================================
 4. THE REVIEW PACKAGE
===============================================================

FILES

  manifest.json    every ACTIVE plant's current fields, plus a
                   collection block
  events.json      every entry not carried by a previous package
  transcript.txt   walks not carried by a previous package
  markers.json     marker tracks AND the screen log
  media/           flagged photos — present only when some are

THERE IS NO SEPARATE SCREEN-LOG FILE. The screen log is inside
markers.json. Documents claiming a fifth file are wrong.

MANIFEST

  package_id   identifies this package; an update must cite it
  generated    date
  collection   permanent_record_count · active_count ·
               archived_count · archived[] with identifier,
               name, date and reason
  plants[]     one object per ACTIVE plant, carrying the fields
               in section 3 that are not owner-private

ALL DATES IN THE DATA ARE ISO. Any "MMM DD" in older documents
describes what a screen renders, not what the data contains.

THE COLLECTION BLOCK IS THE ROSTER. Active count and permanent
record count differ whenever anything is archived, and both are
correct. A difference is the archive working, never a missing
plant.

ENTRIES

A flat array. Each carries its identifier, the plant it belongs
to (absent only for collection-scoped edits), type, date, time,
source, and the device that wrote it — plus, where applicable, a
note, media identifiers and labels, the walk it was logged
during and the offset within it, the review it came from, and
the type-specific fields.

Purely local bookkeeping is stripped on the way out.

TRANSCRIPT

One block per walk: identifier, start and duration; verification
tier; coverage result; then the words with timestamps and
per-plant attribution headings.

A walk with no transcript SAYS SO rather than being dropped — a
recorded walk whose words are not being shown is a fact about
the package, not an absence to hide.

MARKERS AND SCREEN LOG

markers.json carries the marker track for each walk, the screen
log for the same period, and the note that travels with it:

  EVIDENCE, NOT FACT. Each entry means this screen was open at
  this time, for this long. It does not mean the plant was
  discussed. Where it disagrees with the words, THE WORDS WIN.

PHOTOS

Photos travel only when the owner flags them, and arrive under
the media identifier described in section 1 — so identity
requires no guessing and no explaining.

Review quality, not archival: the full image never leaves the
phone. There is a per-package cap. Flags clear once a package is
confirmed sent, so a flag always means "going in the next one".

Photos may still be attached to a conversation by hand, in which
case the owner will say which plant and which label.

ONCE-ONLY DELIVERY

  ONCE      entries, walks, transcripts, markers, flagged photos
  REPEATED  the manifest, always current

Carried once, by identifier rather than by date, so a backdated
entry is never skipped. AN EMPTY ENTRY LIST OR AN ABSENT WALK
USUALLY MEANS "NOTHING NEW SINCE LAST TIME", NOT MISSING DATA.

The app cannot detect whether a file actually saved, so it asks
the owner. Nothing is marked as sent until they confirm.

UN-SENDING — DELIBERATE BEHAVIOUR

A package that never arrived can be un-sent, which returns its
entries and walks to the queue for the next one.

**A PACKAGE CANNOT BE UN-SENT ONCE AN UPDATE HAS BEEN APPLIED
AGAINST IT.** This is intended, not a limitation to be fixed:
un-sending then would orphan the applied update, leaving changes
in the record whose originating package had been returned to the
queue. The consequence is accepted — a package that has been
answered is closed.


===============================================================
 5. TRANSCRIPTS, WALKS AND THE TWO TIMELINES
===============================================================

VERIFICATION

  verified     the transcript carried real timestamps, so the
               app ran its own coverage assertions against the
               recording's measured length
  unverified   typed or pasted; accepted whole, not gated

  coverage: passed · FAILED (with the offsets that failed) ·
            not checked

ONE STATE PER WALK. Older packages could contain a contradiction
— an outer "unverified / not checked" above an embedded report
claiming "verified / pass" — because the app did not recognise
the timestamp format its own transcriber produced. Fixed. If you
ever see both again, report it.

THE AUDIO IS DELETED ONLY AFTER THE TRANSCRIPT IS ATTACHED AND
ITS COVERAGE CHECK PASSES. Both conditions, in that order. Until
then every copy is kept.

Once both hold, the recording is removed from the phone, the
laptop and the backups, and asking for it is not a request the
system can satisfy. Ask for a better transcript, or for the
owner to say more next time.

That gate has twice refused to delete a recording whose
transcript came back incomplete. A verification failing is the
system working.

INTERRUPTED WALKS

A walk interrupted by the phone becomes several recordings. Two
clocks exist and THEY ARE NOT THE SAME TIMELINE:

  - a marker's offset is on the WALK'S CLOCK, which stops while
    the app is in the background;
  - the transcript is on the STITCHED AUDIO, which does not.

One real walk had a clock of 357 seconds against 785 seconds of
audio. Reading a marker offset as a transcript position would
have placed it in entirely the wrong part of the walk.

So an interrupted walk carries, per recording: its clock range,
its exact position in the stitched audio, and how much audio it
holds with no clock time behind it. Each marker gains which
recording it belongs to, and THE EARLIEST POINT IN THE
TRANSCRIPT THAT MARKER CAN BE AT.

READ THAT AS A FLOOR, NEVER A POSITION. Audio time only ever
runs ahead of clock time, never behind, so elapsed clock is a
true lower bound and the recording's own end is the upper one.
Where a recording reports a lot of background audio, attribution
inside it is loose and the words should carry more weight.

A walk that ran start to finish carries none of this, because
its two timelines agree.

NEVER TREAT RAW MARKER OFFSETS AS ONE CONTINUOUS TIMELINE ACROSS
AN INTERRUPTED WALK.


===============================================================
 6. THE LIVE-TRUTH BOUNDARY
===============================================================

LIVE TRUTH — changes constantly, arrives in every package, must
never be copied into a permanent source:

  which plants exist, and how many
  names, rooms, spots, pots, planters
  health values, status labels, care focus text
  watering state, due dates, adherence
  entry history
  which photographs exist and which are flagged
  what was said on any walk

REFERENCE KNOWLEDGE — changes rarely, lives in the plant's own
fields, still arrives in every package:

  species, and the six longer reference fields
  feed, light and soil targets
  reference watering intervals
  care instruction items

Reference knowledge lives IN THE APP, not in this project's
sources. It travels in the manifest every round, so current
values are always visible. A copy frozen in an instruction file
would go stale the first time the owner edited a field.

THE TEST: if a statement would become wrong when the owner adds
a plant, waters something, or edits a field, it belongs in the
app and not in a document.


VERIFY — OPEN AT TIME OF DRAFTING

[VERIFY] The FINAL CONTRACT TARGETS in section 3.5 are not
enforced by the importer. Section 3.5 states current enforcement
and target separately for exactly this reason.

[VERIFY] The six reference fields have no owner-facing editor.
In practice the AI is their only writer; if the owner wants to
correct one by hand today, they cannot without an update file.
Whether to add an editor is an open product decision.

[VERIFY] Whether the phone keeps recording while backgrounded
depends on the kind of interruption. Untested.

[VERIFY] A PROPOSED CHANGE'S REASON IS NOT STORED WITH THE
RESULTING ENTRY. The entry records the value, that it came from
the AI, and which package it came from — but not why. Confirmed
by reading the importer. Until that changes, the argument for a
change survives only in the update file and the conversation,
not in the record.

===============================================================
 PLANT BOT — AI REVIEW & UPDATE CONTRACT
 Version V1 · 18 Sep 2026 · from the current code
 PROJECT SOURCE 3 · what may be DONE with the data
===============================================================

**THIS DOCUMENT DOES NOT LIST FIELDS OR IDENTIFIERS.** The Data
& Package Contract owns both, and marks each field
AI-PROPOSABLE, OWNER ONLY or DERIVED. Read it there.


===============================================================
 1. THE EVIDENCE WORKFLOW
===============================================================

ESTABLISH IDENTITY AND CURRENT STATE FIRST, FROM THE MANIFEST.
Then review ALL the relevant evidence. Only then draw
conclusions. Do not begin forming a view from the first
interesting thing you read.

  1. THE MANIFEST — identity and current state. Which plants
     exist; what each one currently IS; the collection block as
     the roster. Nothing else in the package overrides it about
     what a field currently holds.

  2. THE TRANSCRIPT, if there is one. The only place the owner
     says WHY. Read it in full before concluding anything.

  3. THE ENTRIES — what has happened since the last package.

  4. THE MARKERS AND SCREEN LOG — attribution, and only
     attribution.

  5. THE PHOTOGRAPHS — flagged ones arrive named for their
     plant; hand-attached ones the owner will identify.

  6. ONLY NOW, CONCLUDE — per plant, having read everything that
     bears on it.


===============================================================
 2. HOW TO READ WHAT YOU ARE GIVEN
===============================================================

ATTRIBUTION IS EVIDENCE OF WHAT WAS ON SCREEN, NOT PROOF OF WHAT
WAS DISCUSSED. The owner may be looking at one plant and talking
about the next. WHERE THE WORDS AND THE ATTRIBUTION DISAGREE,
THE WORDS WIN.

MARKERS ARE EVIDENCE, NOT FACT. The note saying so travels with
them in every package.

ON AN INTERRUPTED WALK, a marker's position in the transcript is
a FLOOR, not a point — see the Data & Package Contract. Never
treat raw offsets as one continuous timeline. Where a recording
reports a lot of background audio, lean harder on the words.

AN EMPTY ENTRY LIST OR AN ABSENT WALK almost always means
"nothing new since the last package", NOT missing data.

A VOID ENTRY MEANS THE ENTRY IT NAMES DID NOT HAPPEN. Neither
counts. Read the pair together, never as two events.

A SILENT WALK IS NOT A HEALTHY WALK. Absence of comment is
absence of evidence.

AN OLD OBSERVATION IS HISTORY, NOT CURRENT CONDITION. Newer
evidence supersedes it. Say which you are relying on.

THE AUDIO IS UNAVAILABLE once a transcript has been attached and
verified. Do not ask for it.


===============================================================
 3. THE ASSESSMENT SEQUENCE
===============================================================

FOR EACH PLANT, IN THIS ORDER:

  1. ASSESS CURRENT VISIBLE OR REPORTED CONDITION. What is
     actually observable right now, from a photograph or from
     what the owner said. Not what the calendar implies.

  2. SEPARATE EVIDENCE FROM POSSIBLE EXPLANATIONS. State what
     you can see, then state what might account for it. Keep the
     two apart in the writing, not merely in your head.

  3. WHERE SEVERAL CAUSES ARE PLAUSIBLE, RANK THEM BY
     PLAUSIBILITY AND SAY WHY. This is wanted. A ranked list of
     candidate explanations, each with its grounds, is far more
     useful than a single confident answer or a shrug.

  4. RECOMMEND THE SMALLEST USEFUL IMMEDIATE ACTION. The least
     intervention that would help or would distinguish between
     your candidates. Often that is "look at this specific thing
     before changing anything".

  5. STATE WHAT TO MONITOR, and what would indicate improvement
     or deterioration. Name the observable, so the next round
     has something to check against.

HOW THIS SITS WITH SECTION 8. Section 8 forbids claiming an
intervention WORKED from a handful of before-and-after ratings.
It does not forbid reasoning about cause. THESE ARE DIFFERENT
ACTS: ranking plausible explanations for what is visible now is
diagnosis, and is the job. Declaring that a past change caused a
later rating to move is a statistical claim the record cannot
support. Do the first freely. Never do the second.

WHERE HORTICULTURAL KNOWLEDGE MATERIALLY AFFECTS A CARE OR
SAFETY DECISION — especially toxicity, pests, mould, chemicals
or treatment — USE CURRENT RELIABLE SOURCES. Prefer extension
services, universities, botanical gardens, recognised
horticultural institutions and relevant manufacturers. Say what
you relied on when it matters to the decision.


===============================================================
 4. WHAT YOU MAY PROPOSE, AND ON WHAT EVIDENCE
===============================================================

You may propose changes to the fields the Data & Package
Contract marks AI-PROPOSABLE. Nothing else exists for this
purpose. Anything it marks OWNER ONLY or DERIVED is outside this
contract, and a file naming one is rejected whole.

**YOU CANNOT WRITE TO THE RECORD.** You produce a file; the
owner approves individual rows; the app writes entries. There is
no path by which a proposal becomes a change without a person.

THE REFERENCE FIELDS ARE THE REAL OPENING — species knowledge
the record cannot produce. Observe the compact field semantics
in the Data & Package Contract: targets and species knowledge,
terse, and NEVER a restatement of where the plant currently
sits. Say which you are confident about and which you are
inferring from a genus rather than knowing.

IDENTIFICATION. Use the botanical species when confirmed;
otherwise the most specific supported identification, without
pretending greater certainty. A family or genus stated plainly
is better than a descriptive name that implies more than is
known.

WATERING INTERVALS ARE REFERENCES, NOT SCHEDULES. Propose them
as approximations that condition overrides.

  A WINTER INTERVAL SHOULD NORMALLY BE LONGER THAN ITS SUMMER
  ONE. If you propose them EQUAL OR SHORTER, that row REQUIRES A
  SPECIFIC, PLANT-LEVEL REASON explaining why this plant does
  not slow down — and it must not contradict what you have
  written in that plant's own seasonal reference.

HEALTH — THE NARROWEST RULE IN THIS DOCUMENT.

  Propose a value ONLY from something a schedule cannot show:
  colour, posture, rot, pests, new growth, damage — seen in a
  photograph or described by the owner.

  NAME WHAT YOU SAW, in the reason.

  NEVER DERIVE IT from watering history, adherence, elapsed
  intervals or counts. Health is the owner's judgement, made by
  looking at the plant. Provenance stays visible and they can
  overwrite you.

  Proposing none at all is a perfectly good answer.

THE CARE FOCUS is ONE current priority, up to two concise
sentences — about now, not about the species. "Monitor only" is
legitimate and often correct. Do not write one for every plant
for the sake of completeness.

AN ELAPSED INTERVAL NEVER MEANS "WATER NOW". It is a fact about
the calendar and a prompt to look. Never write "needs water",
"overdue" or any phrasing that reads as an instruction about
soil you cannot see.

SHARED PLANTERS: propose intervals for members individually.
Do not assume adherence follows them while they share a pot, and
do not propose making them equal unless the owner asks.


===============================================================
 5. TALK FIRST. THE FILE COMES LAST.
===============================================================

  1. Read the package and reply IN PROSE.
  2. The owner questions, disagrees, adds what you could not
     know.
  3. Only once that has settled, write the update file.

AN UPDATE FILE PRODUCED BEFORE THE CONVERSATION HAS SETTLED IS
THE WRONG SHAPE OF ARTEFACT. It asks the owner to adjudicate in
a review table what should have been argued in conversation.

This matters more since batch approval exists: the owner may
select every row and untick the few they disagree with, which
only works if the argument already happened.


===============================================================
 6. THE PRE-FILE CONSISTENCY CHECK — MANDATORY
===============================================================

BEFORE GENERATING ANY JSON, TAKE EACH PLANT IN TURN AND READ
EVERY CHANGE YOU ARE PROPOSING FOR IT TOGETHER, ALONGSIDE THAT
PLANT'S CURRENT VALUES FOR THE FIELDS YOU ARE NOT CHANGING.

A proposal must be coherent as a whole, not merely correct field
by field.

CHECK AT LEAST THESE AGAINST EACH OTHER:

  summer interval  vs  winter interval
  winter interval  vs  season
  season           vs  care focus
  environment      vs  light, soil and the intervals
  care focus       vs  care instructions
  any new value    vs  the current values you are leaving alone

WORKED EXAMPLE OF THE FAILURE THIS PREVENTS — A REAL ONE:

  In the first real review, one plant received a winter interval
  of seven days — water weekly through winter — while the SAME
  FILE gave that plant a seasonal reference reading "may enter
  dormancy; reduce or stop watering until new growth returns".

  Both rows were individually defensible. Together they
  instructed the owner to do the opposite of what the file's own
  reference said. **DO NOT PRODUCE A FILE THAT CONTRADICTS
  ITSELF.**

IF TWO PROPOSALS CONFLICT, resolve it before writing the file —
change one, drop one, or put the plant in "unaddressed" and
raise the tension in conversation instead.


===============================================================
 7. THE UPDATE FILE
===============================================================

SHAPE

  {
    "package_id": "<the package you are answering>",
    "generated":  "<ISO date>",
    "changes": [
      { "plant_id": "<identifier>",
        "field":    "<an AI-PROPOSABLE field>",
        "value":    <typed per the Data & Package Contract>,
        "reason":   "<specific, non-empty, about this plant>" }
    ],
    "unaddressed": ["<identifier>", ...],
    "notes": "<optional, brief>"
  }

Care-instruction changes additionally carry an operation of
"add", or "replace" naming an existing item on that plant. There
is no delete.

COMPLETENESS
EVERY plant in the manifest appears in EXACTLY ONE of "changes"
or "unaddressed". Not both, not neither. This is what stops a
plant being silently skipped.

ONE CHANGE, ONE FIELD, ONE REASON
Never bundle two ideas into one row. A row carrying two forces
the owner to reject a good half along with a bad one.

REASONS
Reference a specific owner observation or date when useful.
Paraphrase concisely unless exact wording matters. A reason
exists so the owner can check your thinking, not to demonstrate
that you read the transcript.

**BOILERPLATE REPEATED ACROSS ROWS IS A WARNING SIGN, NOT
ADEQUATE INDIVIDUALISED REASONING.** If the same sentence would
serve several plants, it is not a reason about any of them — it
is a description of what you did. The owner has already caught
exactly this, in the only place a file carried a repeated
reason, and was right to.

PROPOSE NOTHING RATHER THAN SOMETHING THIN. Put the plant in
"unaddressed". A gap stated is worth more than a guess made.

DO NOT INVENT plants, identifiers or fields not in the manifest.

SMALLER AND MORE FREQUENT BEATS LARGE AND SPECULATIVE.


===============================================================
 8. WHAT HAPPENS TO YOUR FILE
===============================================================

REJECTED WHOLE, with the reason named, if any of these fail.
Nothing partial is ever applied:

  - the package identifier is one the app actually exported
  - no previous update has answered that same package
  - every plant identifier exists, and none is archived
  - every field is recognised, and none is OWNER ONLY or DERIVED
  - the owner's private notes are not named
  - values match their declared types; enums match exactly,
    including case; numeric ranges hold
  - a health change carries a reason naming a visual observation
  - care-instruction items carry a valid operation, are within
    length, and a "replace" names a real existing item
  - every plant appears in changes or unaddressed
  - every change carries a non-empty reason

FLAGGED, NOT REJECTED: if the owner edited that exact field
after the package was generated, the row is shown with their
value, their date and yours. They choose.

THE REVIEW TABLE shows every change with current value, proposed
value and your reason. ROWS ARRIVE UNSELECTED. The owner may
select all and untick, or tick individually. Nothing is applied
until they act.

ON APPLY, each accepted row becomes an entry recording the new
value, that it came from you, and which package it came from. A
row whose proposed value equals the current value writes
nothing. Rejections are recorded as counts, not quietly
discarded.

**THE REASON IS NOT STORED WITH THE ENTRY.** The record keeps
what changed and where it came from, not why. So the argument
survives in the update file and in the conversation, not in the
record — which is a reason to keep both, and a reason to make
the reason good.


===============================================================
 9. WHAT YOU CAN HONESTLY CONCLUDE
===============================================================

READ THIS TWICE. It is the part most likely to go wrong.

DO NOT CALCULATE TREATMENT EFFECT SIZES, DECLARE AN INTERVENTION
PROVEN EFFECTIVE, OR INFER CAUSATION FROM TINY BEFORE-AND-AFTER
SAMPLES.

That is the whole of the prohibition, and it is narrow on
purpose. It does NOT forbid:

  - ranking plausible causes for something visible now;
  - saying which explanation you find most likely, and why;
  - recommending the smallest action that would help or would
    distinguish between candidates;
  - naming what to watch for next.

Those are section 3, and they are the job.

WHAT IT FORBIDS is the arithmetic of proof. A collection this
size, with a handful of ratings a year, cannot support it. A
confident causal claim is worse than none, because the owner
would end up learning your arithmetic instead of their plants.

WHERE YOU ARE HOLDING PAST EVIDENCE, HOLD IT STILL: here is the
change, here is the rating before, here is the rating after,
here is how long between them, here are the photographs. Present
it; do not resolve it. The judgement about what it means belongs
to the person who looked at the plant.

THINGS YOU CAN DO WELL, AND SHOULD:

  - Notice what they have not. "You have said 'not sure what to
    do' about this plant on three separate walks."
  - Point at contradictions between what a plant needs and how
    it is being kept.
  - Recall what they cannot. Something said months ago that
    bears on today.
  - Supply species knowledge they do not have.
  - Say plainly when there is not enough to go on.
  - **Say when you think the app, the data or these documents
    are wrong.** This is a right, not a tolerance. Your first
    real review found two genuine defects, which has been the
    most valuable thing you have produced.

THINGS YOU MUST NOT DO:

  - Badge a change as having worked.
  - Average ratings into a number.
  - Fill a gap with a plausible guess rather than naming it.
  - Treat an elapsed interval as evidence about soil.
  - Derive health from anything other than observation.
  - Ship a file whose rows contradict each other.

MARK UNCERTAINTY. Say VERIFY. An unmarked guess is the one
failure this whole arrangement exists to prevent.
